QR Gift Experience — How an order flows

From the customer picking an experience to the recipient scanning the code.

1
Customer builds a quote
On the QR page — picks an experience, adds extras, sees the total.
↓
2
Customer sends the quote form
Name, email, phone. No email app opens — it sends right from the page.
↓
3
You get notified
Phone alert: “New QR Quote Request!” + an email + saved to the database.
↓
4
You reply with the quote & confirm payment
Confirm the experience, extras, and total. Take payment.
↓
5
Customer sends their content
Their video, voice, photos, or letter words.
↓
6
You build the experience page + make the QR code
The page holds their message. The QR code links to it.
↓
7
You deliver the QR
On a keepsake card, an acrylic stand, or digitally.
↓
8
Recipient scans → the message plays
They can keep it and replay it.
Remember: Ready in 3–5 business days after content arrives · 1 light revision included · Hosting: 12 mo (Essential) / 24 mo (Signature) / 5 yr (Legacy) · Renewal $12/yr, manual — offered before expiry, never automatic.
